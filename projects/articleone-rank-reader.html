ArticleOnePartners Prior Art Rank Reader Tool

ArticleOnePartners recruited public researchers to find prior art associated with patents.

The team that reviewed those submissions needed a tool to support their investigation and review process.

My work at ArticleOnePartners was mostly concepts and prototyping across a number of projects. The prior art rank reader was a single-page application that let submissions be analyzed with a tag comparison tool: it displayed the relevant section of text for a submission and let the reviewer enter a status and supporting information. Following prototyping, I built the initial layout and contributed to the JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3 and PHP development.
