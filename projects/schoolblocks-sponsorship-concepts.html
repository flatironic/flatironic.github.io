SchoolBlocks Sponsorship Concepts

SchoolBlocks is a CMS for schools. The sponsorship system was conceived to provide additional school funding, with a percentage going to SchoolBlocks.

The specifications and design for the sponsorship program had to be defined before development could start. Details that needed defining included:
  • The process of signing sponsors up
  • The management of sponsors by the school district and SchoolBlocks
  • Sponsors' management of their own sponsorships
  • The process of approval
  • Sponsorship visibility on school websites
  • Sponsor editing of their web presence

Using Photoshop to create concepts and InVision to produce a prototype of the application, I was able to refine and define both the design and the business approach through collaboration.
