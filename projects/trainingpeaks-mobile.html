TrainingPeaks Mobile Application

TrainingPeaks is an advanced training log for endurance athletes. As Creative Director I provided concepts and wireframes while continuing to develop and maintain most of their marketing website and e-commerce in ASP.NET.

TrainingPeaks needed a mobile solution.

Working with one other developer, we built a service-based HTML5 app carrying the most relevant features of the full version of TrainingPeaks. It was a single-page web app that reached into iPhone features to extend its usability, including a barcode scanner that automatically entered nutrition data for food.

The foundation set by that JavaScript and HTML5 work led the way to the rebuild of the browser-based TrainingPeaks application that followed.
