CPE Today — E-Learning Platform

Earning continuing professional education credit is a regulated process: the platform has to deliver the content, prove the learner completed it, and produce the paperwork that stands up afterwards.

I designed and developed the e-learning platform that delivers course content and handles registration for live events. It covers exams, certificate generation, account management and the tracking of CPE credits across a learner's history.

Built in Vue against ASP.NET services, as part of a small team where my work ran from visual concepts and feature design through to the full-stack implementation.
